About us

Farming that produces, adds value and creates jobs

Our vision

To transform agriculture from subsistence into productive, value-added, job-creating agribusiness, while empowering youth and women through decent jobs, entrepreneurship and skills.

Our mission

To provide high-quality farm inputs, value-addition services and professional agronomic support that raise productivity and profit, while promoting sustainable farming and building farmers' skills.

What we do

Our services

Crop production

We grow sunflower and maize ourselves, and show farmers what good practice looks like in the field.

Quality seeds and fertilizer

Certified seed and fertilizer from the companies we partner with, sold from our shop in Kibaigwa.

Value addition

Sunlush sunflower oil and maize flour, so the value of the harvest stays in Kibaigwa.

Farmer education and advice

Shamba Darasa training on better yields, correct input use and sustainable, climate-smart farming.

Why Kibaigwa

In the heart of the sunflower belt

312,000 t

Sunflower oil produced in Tanzania in 2024, more than any other African country.

42%

Of the country's sunflower comes from Dodoma, Singida and neighbouring regions.

On the highway

Kibaigwa sits on the Morogoro–Dodoma road, a grain trading town between the farms and Dar es Salaam.
