Why Kibaigwa

In the heart of the sunflower belt

312,000 t

Sunflower oil produced in Tanzania in 2024, more than any other African country.

42%

Of the country's sunflower comes from Dodoma, Singida and neighbouring regions.

On the highway

Kibaigwa sits on the Morogoro–Dodoma road, a grain trading town between the farms and Dar es Salaam.
